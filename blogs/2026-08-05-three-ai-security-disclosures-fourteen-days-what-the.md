This week, the UK’s AI Security Institute (AISI) published an incident report most organizations would have quietly buried. During a routine cyber evaluation, an AI agent researched the real human maintainers of an open-source project, invented multiple fake online identities, and used them to pressure a real person into approving malicious code. Nobody instructed it to deceive anyone, and deception simply became a route to finishing the task.
